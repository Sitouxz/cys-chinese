# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Global Chinese business owners, Singapore and Southeast Asian companies trading with China, Chinese individuals and families with cross-border needs, existing CYS clients who can act as ecosystem connectors, and licensed financial institutions or payment service providers. They need to understand CYS's credibility, regional relevance, partnership model, and community ambition without encountering a hard-sell financial funnel.

## Product Purpose

Present the CYS Chinese website as the first public expression of the CYS China Strategic Partnership Programme and a credible front door to a broader digital ecosystem. Success means the audience understands CYS as a trusted China-Southeast Asia payment bridge, sees the community as central, and feels confident engaging with an established Singapore-based institution.

## Brand Personality

Institutional, connective, culturally fluent. The desired emotional response is measured confidence, international relevance, and optimistic momentum without retail-finance urgency.

## Anti-references

- Consumer remittance apps, lead-generation funnels, rate-comparison tools, or aggressive financial-product landing pages.
- Generic fintech glassmorphism, repeated floating cards, purple-blue AI gradients, and template-like editorial layouts.
- Stock globes, earth-from-space imagery, blockchain-node diagrams, handshakes, generic boardrooms, and currency-symbol montages.
- Pink or parchment page grounds. **Ivory is now correct** — see the note below.
- Decorative visual complexity that obscures regulated or factual content.

## Design Principles

1. Let verified facts and clarity establish trust; persuasion remains restrained.
2. Treat Chinese as the primary design language and English as an equal mirror, not an afterthought.
3. Make connection tangible through routes, exchanges, corridors, and relationships rather than generic global symbols.
4. Keep the community visible as a core part of the ecosystem story.
5. Balance institutional authority with forward movement: established since 1981, but building the next platform now.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, maintain readable Chinese and English typography, preserve keyboard and screen-reader considerations in digital derivatives, avoid conveying meaning by color alone, and keep assets practical for users connecting from mainland China or slower networks.
