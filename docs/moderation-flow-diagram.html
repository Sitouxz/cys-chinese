  • 未通过Not approved — category + rewrite hint, editable, resubmits to the queue

Every state change sends an email. Silence after submitting is the biggest risk in a pre-publication design — it reads as a broken site.

Why the word list runs first STAGE 1 → 2

The scan is instant, free, and legally legible — "matched 逃汇, 《外汇管理条例》" is a defensible answer to a regulator; a model's opinion is not. It also means high-risk posts are rejected before any AI call is paid for.

The AI stage exists for the word list's blind spot: negation — 反洗钱 contains 洗钱 — and posts that are sensitive without using any listed word.
