Locale scheme
Locale is the first path segment — /zh/about/history and /en/about/history. / redirects to /zh. Route segments stay English in both locales, so no percent-encoded Chinese URLs. The switcher swaps only the locale segment and keeps the rest of the path.
Nav taxonomy — resolved
The deck's footer filed 伙伴故事 and 星威论坛 under 华商社群; the header filed them under 中新合作走廊 and 企业合作. This diagram adopts the header taxonomy as canonical — it matches the URL tree. 华商社群 becomes a footer heading, not a page.
Phase split
Everything in white and navy ships in the Phase 1 demo, early October. The green forum cluster is Phase 2, around December. Auth routes are built in Phase 1 so the header links aren't dead, but gate nothing until the forum exists.
