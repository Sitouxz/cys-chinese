WHAT THIS TIMELINE ASSUMESPLEASE READ
Two months covers the website and the forum, which is tighter than our original plan of building the forum after the demo. It is achievable, and these are the three things it depends on:
  1. Sign-offs inside three working days. Each stage feeds the next, so a week spent waiting on approval is a week added to the end. The four points where we need you are marked on the timeline above.
  2. One round of feedback per stage. We have allowed for one proper round of comments on the wireframes and one on the designs. A second round is completely fine — it simply moves the launch date, and we will tell you by how much on the day.
  3. The forum launches with the essentials. Accounts, posting, comments, the review queue, search and 有用/举报 are all in. Extras such as image uploads, private messaging and following other members come after launch, once the forum has real members using it.
Some of the forum groundwork runs alongside the website build rather than after it, which is what makes the two months work — the website and the forum are one system, not two.
