1981年启航于新加坡Founded in Singapore, 1981

连接中国与东南亚的可信支付桥梁，启迪跨境商业新可能。 A trusted payment bridge connecting China and Southeast Asia — inspiring new possibilities in cross-border business.

星威环球（CYS Global Remit）是新加坡金融管理局持牌大型支付机构，40余年来专注中新走廊的跨境支付与结算服务。

CYS Global Remit is a MAS-licensed Major Payment Institution, serving the China–Singapore corridor for over four decades.

年经手资金流动超过 22 亿美元 · 服务亚太数以千计企业与家庭 USD 2.2B+ processed annually · Trusted by thousands across Asia-Pacific
