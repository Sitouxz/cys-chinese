The brief
01 — What we are making

A positioning site, 不是销售漏斗

The Chinese site is the first step of the CYS China Strategic Partnership Programme and the starting point of a broader digital ecosystem platform.

It exists to do two things: open the Chinese market, and build a Chinese business community around a forum where members share resources and find each other.

"A trusted payment bridge connecting China and Southeast Asia."

连接中国与东南亚的可信支付桥梁，启迪跨境商业新可能。

It is

  • An institutional credibility statement
  • A community front door
  • Bilingual, CN-first
  • Visibly international

It is not

  • A product sales page
  • A lead-generation funnel
  • A rate-comparison tool
  • A consumer remittance app

Financial marketing is regulated in China. Restraint here is a compliance requirement, not a stylistic preference.

03 — Design principles

Four rules that settle arguments

PRINCIPLE 01

Credibility over persuasion

Forty-five years, an MAS licence, USD 2.2bn a year. The facts do the selling. No urgency devices, no countdown, no "get started now". Whitespace and precision read as institutional; density and hard CTAs read as retail.

PRINCIPLE 02

Chinese first, not translated

The CN layout is the design; EN is the mirror. Line length, leading, and letter-spacing are set for Chinese and adapted for English — never the reverse. If a layout only works in one language, it is not finished.

PRINCIPLE 03

Connection is the visual idea

The client asked for a better expression of global and connection than stock earth imagery. Bridges, corridors, trade routes, threads between points — the corridor is the story, not the planet.

PRINCIPLE 04

The community is visible

The boss's priority is the community. Forum posts appear on the homepage and link through. A visitor should see people using this within the first screen and a half — not discover a forum buried in the nav.

05
Direction
04 — Colour, two alternates

If Corridor isn't right

Institutional 稳健

Cool greys replace the cream. Reads more like a bank and less like an editorial brand — safest with conservative FI partners, but gives up the warmth that makes the site feel like a community rather than a counterparty.

Bridge 桥梁

Adds a terracotta drawn from Chinese architectural red, used for the corridor and milestone sections. Warmest and most distinctive of the three — but red carries weight in this market and needs a deliberate hand.

Our recommendation is Corridor. It is closest to what CYS already mocked up, so it needs the least internal selling, and the warm ground differentiates without taking a risk on red.

05 — Typography

One system, two writing systems

Display — headlines
连接中国与东南亚的可信支付桥梁
A Trusted Payment Bridge
Noto Serif SC + Latin serif companion · 600 · CN tracking +0.06em
Body — running text
星威环球成立于1981年，总部设于新加坡。
Founded in 1981, headquartered in Singapore.
CN system stack · EN self-hosted sans · 400

The constraint that shapes everything

A full CJK webfont is 3–10 MB — unusable, especially for visitors on slow connections into China. So: CN body runs on the system stack (PingFang SC → Microsoft YaHei → Noto Sans SC), and any display face is subset to the ~500 characters actually used in headings.

Same component, different metrics

Chinese needs looser leading and wider tracking than English at the same size; English needs tighter tracking and a shorter measure. These are set as tokens scoped by language, not forked components.

:lang(zh) → leading 1.4 / tracking .08em
:lang(en) → leading 1.15 / tracking .02em

06 — Imagery

Express connection, 而非地球

The client's note on the homepage mockup, verbatim: "背景我想要表达是 global and connection。希望可以有更好的方式可以表达 connection" — they want a better way to express connection than the stock earth-from-space image. This is an open creative brief, not a locked direction.

Reach for

  • Corridors, bridges, routes — the CN–SG corridor made literal
  • Ports, containers, night freight — real trade, not abstract finance
  • Singapore and Chinese cityscapes at dusk, held in one frame
  • Real CYS people, office, and events wherever they exist

Avoid

  • Earth-from-space and glowing network globes
  • Handshake stock photography and generic boardrooms
  • Floating currency symbols, rising arrows, candlestick charts
  • Purple-to-blue gradients and default AI-generated aesthetics

Four partner cards on the corridor page and both partnership stories are currently placeholders. Real logos and named stories are outstanding from the client.

System
09 — Non-negotiable constraints

Design inside these

TECHNICAL

Reachable from China

No Google Fonts, Maps, Analytics, reCAPTCHA, or YouTube. Every asset self-hosted and served from our own domain.

TECHNICAL

Weight budget

Connections into the mainland are slow even when they work. The hero is the single biggest risk — art-direct for a small file, don't retouch for one.

TECHNICAL

No CAPTCHA

Forms are protected by honeypot and rate limiting, so the design carries no puzzle widget anywhere.

EXPERIENCE

Accessible

AA contrast minimum. Jade on cream fails for body text — accent only. Every interactive element reachable by keyboard.

REGULATORY

No regulated claims

No rates, no returns, no "best price". Figures are historical facts, stated plainly and attributed.

STRUCTURAL

CMS-shaped

Copy is client-editable in both languages, so no layout may depend on a fixed character count.
