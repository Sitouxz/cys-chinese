## 7. Visual direction (from deck mockups)

**Colour palette as mocked up — NOT YET APPROVED. Client has explicitly asked to confirm the colour scheme before work starts.**

| Role | Observed in mockups |
|---|---|
| Hero | Full-bleed dark navy / near-black earth-from-space photography, sun flare on the horizon, city lights |
| Primary brand blue | Deep royal/navy blue — used for the corridor section block and the currency ticker bar |
| Background | Warm cream / off-white |
| Accent | CYS logo green + light blue (existing brand mark) |
| Header | Translucent over hero; blue when scrolled/on interior pages |
| Buttons | Pill-shaped, light fill with dark text (hero CTA), outlined pill for secondary |
| Typography | CN: standard sans (SC). EN: geometric sans. Headline set in a wide, letter-spaced treatment |
| Floating element | Circular green button bottom-right on every screen — presumably WhatsApp/WeChat quick contact |

**Imagery:** globe/space (connection, global), classical bank architecture (institutional trust), handshake / document-signing (partnership), sky. All currently stock or placeholder; the four corridor cards are placeholder CYS logos.

**Client's own note on the hero:** wants a **better** way to express "connection" than the current earth image — this is an open creative brief, not a locked direction.

**Tone constraint:** must read as an **international** financial institution, not a consumer remittance app and not a lead-gen page. Financial marketing is regulated in China; restrained, institutional, credibility-forward.
