### Long-form article — 星威里程碑：四十余载，一路同行

**CN** (deck marks this "content for history")

> 1981年，"CYS"这个名字第一次出现在新加坡的金融版图上。凭借新加坡金融管理局（MAS）颁发的正式牌照，CYS品牌自1981年创立以来，持续为北亚和南亚走廊提供包括人民币在内的多种亚洲小众货币的流动性支持，为跨境支付和本地转账业务打下了坚实的根基。这一年，星威正式成为新加坡持牌金融机构，从此踏上了服务中小企业跨境汇款的征程。
>
> 2011年，公司完成了一次重要的品牌升级——公司原名为CYS Express Exchange，正式更名为CYS Global Remit。这不仅仅是一个名字的改变，更象征着企业从早期的兑换服务，迈向了更专业、更国际化的全球汇款平台。
>
> 2020年，突如其来的疫情改变了整个世界的工作和消费方式。星威顺势而为，开启了居家办公的新时代，同时大力推广"无现金"交易，帮助客户在动荡的环境中依然能够安全、便捷地完成跨境资金往来。
>
> 2025年，随着业务规模不断扩大，公司从Shenton House搬迁至位于Cecil Street的GB Building，为团队和客户提供了更完善的办公与服务环境，也标志着星威迈入了新的发展阶段。
>
> 展望2030年，星威将继续"寻找更多的合作机会"——凭借每年超过22亿美元的交易处理规模，其中一半以上用于跨境支付，公司将持续深耕东南亚与大中华区市场，携手更多金融机构与合作伙伴，把便捷、安全的跨境支付服务带给更多中小企业和个人用户。
>
> 从1981年的一纸牌照，到如今服务每年超过4万笔汇款交易、涉及20亿美元客户资金的区域性支付机构，星威的故事，是一个关于坚持与成长的故事。

**EN — CYS Milestones: Forty-Plus Years, Moving Forward Together**

> In 1981, the name "CYS" first appeared on Singapore's financial map. Backed by an official licence issued by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS), the CYS brand has, since its founding in 1981, continuously provided liquidity support for a range of Asian niche currencies — including the RMB — along the North Asia and South Asia corridors, laying a solid foundation for its cross-border payment and domestic remittance business. This was the year CYS officially became a licensed financial institution in Singapore, marking the start of its journey serving cross-border remittances for SMEs.
>
> In 2011, the company completed a major brand upgrade — rebranding from its original name, CYS Express Exchange, to CYS Global Remit. This was more than just a name change; it symbolized the company's evolution from an early-stage currency exchange service into a more professional, internationalized global remittance platform.
>
> In 2020, the sudden onset of the pandemic transformed how the world worked and spent. CYS adapted swiftly, embracing the new era of remote work while actively promoting "cashless" transactions — helping clients complete cross-border fund transfers safely and conveniently even amid a turbulent environment.
>
> In 2025, as its business continued to grow, the company relocated from Shenton House to GB Building on Cecil Street, providing an improved working and service environment for both its team and its clients, and marking CYS's entry into a new phase of development.
>
> Looking ahead to 2030, CYS will continue to "seek out more partnership opportunities" — building on an annual transaction volume of over USD 2.2 billion, more than half of which goes toward cross-border payments, the company will keep deepening its presence in the Southeast Asian and Greater China markets, working alongside more financial institutions and partners to bring convenient, secure cross-border payment services to even more SMEs and individual users.
>
> From a single licence in 1981 to a regional payment institution now processing over 40,000 remittance transactions and handling USD 2 billion in client funds each year — the CYS story is one of persistence and growth.
