No dislike button. Your word list rates 骗子 and 被骗了 as risky because public accusations against businesses are a liability. A dislike counter is that same risk with a number attached — live instantly, never reviewed, sitting on someone else's listing. And since the businesses here compete, it is a one-click way to push a rival out of 热门话题. Use 举报/Report instead.

Why the fields above the text matter. All four sample posts follow the same shape, so we capture it at entry rather than as free prose. That is what makes 检索用户/产品/服务 a real query instead of keyword soup, makes filters work, and gives the moderation check a cleaner signal — a post whose fields say apparel manufacturer seeking brands but whose body discusses 换汇 is visibly incongruent.

Every state change sends an email. With posts checked before they appear, a poster hits submit and sees nothing — so silence is the single biggest UX risk in this design. It reads as a broken site, and they don't come back.

ONE DECISION FOR CYSDISLIKES — YES / NO
You asked what we propose for likes, comments and dislikes. Likes yes, comments yes, dislikes no. Renaming the like to 有用/Useful gives you a signal that means something on a matching board — "this supplier is relevant to me" — and gives 热门话题 something real to rank on, which it currently has no source for. Dislikes we would leave out:
  1. It publishes an unreviewed negative judgement onto a business listing — the opposite of the pre-publication rule you asked for everywhere else.
  2. Your own word list treats accusations against businesses as a risk (骗子, 被骗了). This reintroduces that risk as a product feature.
  3. The members compete with each other, so the motive to abuse it is built in.
If you want a negative signal anyway, the least-harmful form is a private downvote that feeds ranking and is never shown — but we would not ship even that at launch, because a poster whose listing quietly stops appearing cannot be given an explanation.
Not a discussion forum THE CONSTRAINT

Every feature above is judged on one question: does it help a real business get found by the right counterparty? The deck's four sample posts are all the same shape — "I am this business, I want to meet that kind of partner" — so this is a business directory with discussion attached, not a message board.

Reddit-style engagement mechanics optimise for attention on opinions instead, which is why we've reframed the like and dropped the dislike rather than importing the usual set wholesale.

Open questions NEED A DECISION

1 · Dislikes — happy with the recommendation? Only item here that changes the data model.

2 · Read access — we suggest posts publicly readable, posting and contact details login-gated. Public read is what makes the forum work as a shopfront and findable at all.

3 · Forum language — Chinese-only or bilingual? Every label here has a CN/EN pair, but the word list is Chinese-only, so English posts would be only half-checked.

4 · Comments — reviewed before they appear too, or posts only? Assumed yes above.

5 · Verified badge — does CYS verify a company, or is it self-declared?
